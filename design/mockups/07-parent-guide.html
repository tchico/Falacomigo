When they answer you in English

Parents usually respond in one of a few ways: carry on and accept the English, say it back in Portuguese, guess what the child meant, or say they didn't understand.4 Fathers of children who understand but don't speak carried on about 93% of the time, and the occasional prompt made little difference.5 Nudges work when they're a steady habit.

Say it back: “Queres água? Diz: quero água.” Then wait.
Guess, as a question: “Queres ir ao parque? Diz-me em português.”
Don't understand, on purpose: “Como? Não percebi…” Use it lightly, then give the words.
Try this week: every time they answer in English, say it back in Portuguese and pause for three seconds.
Sources
4 · Lanza (1997), Language Mixing in Infant Bilingualism 5 · Nakamura (2018), Parents' use of discourse strategies with receptive bilingual children
