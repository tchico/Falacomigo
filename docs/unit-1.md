# Unit 1 — Olá! Me and my family

Unit 1 is the first stop, the back garden: Gui crash-lands and the kids have to introduce themselves. It has 12 phrases, 3 scenes and 7 missions. The machine-readable version the app loads is [`content/units/unit-01.json`](../content/units/unit-01.json).

## Phrases

There are 12 phrases. The 6-year-old needs the 7 marked "Both", and the 8-year-old also learns to ask questions back. Phrases they almost certainly know already start at rung 3, and new question forms start at rung 1.

| ID | Phrase (pt-PT) | English | Also accept | Who | Start rung |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P01 | Olá! | Hello! | olá Gui, oi | Both | 3 |
| P02 | Bom dia / Boa tarde / Boa noite | Good morning / afternoon / night | bom dia pai, boa noite Gui | Both | 2 |
| P03 | Chamo-me ___. | My name is ___. | o meu nome é ___, eu sou o/a ___, sou o/a ___ | Both | 2 |
| P04 | Como te chamas? | What's your name? | como é que te chamas, qual é o teu nome, e tu? | 8yo | 1 |
| P05 | Tenho ___ anos. | I'm ___ years old. | eu tenho ___ anos, ___ anos | Both | 2 |
| P06 | Quantos anos tens? | How old are you? | e tu quantos anos tens, quantos anos é que tens | 8yo | 1 |
| P07 | Este é o meu ___ / Esta é a minha ___. | This is my ___. | é o meu ___, é a minha ___, este é o/a [name] | 8yo | 2 |
| P08 | Eu moro na Irlanda. | I live in Ireland. | moro na Irlanda, vivo na Irlanda, eu vivo na Irlanda | 8yo | 2 |
| P09 | Obrigado! / Obrigada! | Thank you! | either form from either child, muito obrigado/a | Both | 3 |
| P10 | Como estás? — Estou bem. | How are you? — I'm fine. | bem, estou bem obrigado/a, estou ótimo/a, mais ou menos | Both (6yo answers only) | 2 |
| P11 | Anda cá! | Come here! | vem cá, anda, anda Gui | 8yo | 2 |
| P12 | Adeus! Até amanhã! | Bye! See you tomorrow! | adeus, até amanhã, até logo, xau, tchau | Both | 3 |

**Words for the blanks:** o pai, a mãe, o irmão, a irmã, o bebé, a avó, o avô, o cão, o gato, and the kids' own names, which are added in the parent zone (FR-28). For numbers, only seis, sete, oito and nove are needed for now.

**Grammar in passing:** P07 introduces o/a (este é o meu / esta é a minha) without explaining it. It's accepted even if they get it wrong, and Gui repeats it back correctly.

## Scenes

There are three scenes of 6–8 minutes each. Each beat is one spoken turn. Lines in *italics* are Gui's, and the → shows what the child should say.

### Scene 1.1 — Quem és tu? (Who are you?)

A storm, a thud, and a dizzy seagull on the trampoline.

1. *"Ai, ai, ai… onde estou?"* ("Ouch… where am I?") The child taps Gui, and Gui is waiting to be greeted → **P01** Olá!
2. *"Olá! Como te chamas?"* ("Hello! What's your name?") → **P03** Chamo-me ___.
3. *"Que nome bonito! Quantos anos tens?"* ("What a lovely name! How old are you?") → **P05** Tenho ___ anos.
4. *"Eu tenho… hmm… cem anos!"* ("I'm… hmm… a hundred!") Gui is joking.
    - **8yo** → **P04** E tu, como te chamas? ("And what's your name?"), then Gui answers *"Chamo-me Gui!"*
    - **6yo:** Gui just tells them his name.
5. Gui shivers → **P10** Como estás? / *"Estou com frio!"* ("I'm cold!"). The 6yo is instead asked *"Como estás?"* and answers.
6. **Cliffhanger:** Gui notices a photo in the kitchen window: *"Quem são aqueles?"* ("Who are they?")

### Scene 1.2 — A família (The family)

Gui presses his beak against a family photo (the real photo, uploaded in the parent zone).

1. *"Quem é este?"* ("Who's this?") pointing at Dad → **P07** Este é o meu pai! (the 6yo can just say "o pai")
2. Gui mistakes Mum for a seagull (*"É uma gaivota?"*, "Is she a seagull?") → child: Não! É a minha mãe! This gag is where the laugh comes from.
3. One beat for each sibling in the photo, using the names from the parent zone → Este é o meu irmão / Esta é a minha irmã.
4. Gui points at the child in the photo → Sou eu! ("That's me!") plus **P05** Tenho ___ anos.
5. *"E a tua avó? Onde está?"* ("And your granny? Where is she?") This sets up Unit 7 and the grandparent mission.
6. **Cliffhanger:** Gui sees a Lisbon postcard on the fridge and gasps: *"A minha casa!"* ("My home!")

### Scene 1.3 — Onde estou? (Where am I?)

Gui is sad and far from home.

1. *"Onde estou? Isto é Lisboa?"* ("Where am I? Is this Lisbon?") → **8yo P08** Eu moro na Irlanda! / **6yo** "Não! Irlanda!"
2. Gui droops → **P10** Como estás? / *"Estou triste… tenho saudades de casa."* ("I'm sad… I miss home.")
3. The child offers to help. Gui brightens up: *"A sério? Obrigado!"* ("Really? Thank you!") → the child answers, and Gui prompts **P09** by thanking them for a biscuit.
4. **8yo** → **P11** Anda cá! ("Come here!") calls Gui over to the map. **6yo** taps the map, then says *"Anda cá, Gui!"* with the echo prompt.
5. The map opens with a dotted line from the garden to Lisbon, and the ferry is the first stop.
6. Bedtime → **P02** Boa noite! + **P12** Até amanhã! This ends the unit and unlocks Stop 2, the ferry.

### Warm-up pool

Used on review sessions as quick questions from Gui: P01, P02, P03, P05, P10, and "Quem é este?" with a random family photo.

## Missions to Dad

There's one mission a day for 7 days. These are also the cards for the Phase 0 paper test: draw the picture, write the phrase underneath, and add a sticker when it's done.

| Day | Mission card (shown to the child) | 6-year-old says | 8-year-old says | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Diz olá ao pai como disseste ao Gui (Say hello to Dad like you did to Gui) | Olá, pai! Chamo-me ___. | Olá, pai! Chamo-me ___ e tenho ___ anos. | Straight after the session |
| 2 | Bom dia, pai! | Bom dia, pai! | Bom dia, pai! Como estás? + answers when Dad asks back | Next morning at breakfast |
| 3 | Apresenta alguém ao pai (Introduce someone to Dad) | Esta é a mãe! | Este é o meu irmão, ___. Tem ___ anos. | Any time with someone in the room |
| 4 | Pergunta ao pai quantos anos ele tem (Ask Dad how old he is) | *(6yo version:)* Tell Dad your age: Tenho ___ anos! | Quantos anos tens, pai? | Any time. Joke answers from Dad are encouraged ("Tenho cem anos!") |
| 5 | Boa noite, pai! | Boa noite, pai! | Boa noite, pai! Até amanhã! | Bedtime |
| 6 | Juntos! (Together!) Introduce each other to Dad as if Dad were Gui | Este é o meu irmão! | Esta é a minha irmã. Tem ___ anos. | When both kids are around |
| 7 | Bonus: a video call with the avó | Olá, avó! | Olá, avó! Como estás? Eu estou bem. | Whenever a call is planned |

**Stars guide:** 1 star for trying (any Portuguese at all), 2 for the target phrase, and 3 for doing it without the card or adding something extra. Missions 2 and 5 happen at set times of day, which helps turn them into habits.

"Brother" and "sister" are examples. Swap them for the real siblings in the parent zone.

## Dad's part

Two jobs: record about 40 short clips once (around 20 minutes), and reply in a particular way during missions.

### What to record

- [ ] The 12 phrases from the table, said naturally and slightly slower than normal. Record both Obrigado and Obrigada, and all three of Bom dia, Boa tarde and Boa noite.
- [ ] P03, P05 and P07 filled in for each child: "Chamo-me [name]", "Tenho [age] anos", and "Este é o meu irmão, [name]" / "Esta é a minha irmã, [name]".
- [ ] The words for the blanks: o pai, a mãe, o irmão, a irmã, o bebé, a avó, o avô, and the numbers seis to nove.
- [ ] Optional: Gui's lines in a silly seagull voice. Otherwise a pt-PT text-to-speech voice reads them.
- [ ] A family photo and a photo of the grandparents for Scene 1.2.

Record in a quiet room, holding the phone about 20 cm away. Name each clip with its ID (for example `P03-child1.m4a`) and put it in `content/audio/unit-01/`.

### How to respond during missions

- **Answer in Portuguese only, and keep it short and warm.** "Bom dia, filho! Estou bem, e tu?" Then pause and wait for them to answer.
- **If they switch to English, act puzzled.** Try "Como? Não percebi…" and point to the card. Only do this once or twice, and then give them the phrase.
- **Repeat back, don't correct.** If they say "Este é a mãe", answer "Ah, esta é a mãe! Olá, mãe!" and never say "no, it's esta".
- **Always add one small extra question** once they've done the target phrase ("E tu, como estás?"). That extra turn is the real practice.
- **Give the stars straight away.** Right after is when it motivates them most.
